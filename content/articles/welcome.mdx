## What This Is

Crude Academy is a plain-English introduction to oil refineries, with
topics drawn from my own experience working in them. The articles and videos
range from broad overarching themes down to the specific, sometimes
overlooked, technologies and practices that make a plant run.

## How It's Organized

The content is split into three modules. **Module 1** is the foundation: a
light introduction to the chemistry of hydrocarbons, followed by what comes
into the refinery (crude) and what comes out (gasoline, diesel, and other
products). The chemistry will make more complex refinery topics easier to
understand, because you will have a feel for how to describe and talk about
hydrocarbon molecules.

With that foundation in place, the other two modules go deeper into how a
refinery actually works. **Module 2** covers the refinery processes
themselves: distillation, cracking, reforming, hydrotreating, and how the units
fit together. **Module 3** is the hardware side: pumps, compressors, valves,
instrumentation, electrical, and the inspection and maintenance that keep the
plant running safely.
