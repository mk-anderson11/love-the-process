I have worked around soft starters, but never took the time to understand why they are used or how they work.

I watched an excellent YouTube video that breaks it down: [__https://lnkd.in/eV5yUdrb__](https://lnkd.in/eV5yUdrb)

Here's my 35,000 FT summary:

⚡ Some electric motors need a lot of electricity when accelerating to their normal operating speed.

⚡ The surge of current (inrush current) and resulting torque can damage motor components.

✏️ Soft starters gradually increase the current and torque during start up and shut down.

✏️ The benefit of a soft starter is smoother, safer, more gradual start ups and shut downs. This can save money by preventing damage to motors and electrical equipment.

As you may have guessed, I recently had an issue with a soft starter at work. The pump wouldn't start!

Electricians traced the issue back to the soft starter, which needed to be replaced. The pump promptly started up. This was a great outcome, but I realized I couldn't explain the purpose of a soft starter.

Here's to learning.
