Desalter effluent is one type of process water that must be treated in the refinery wastewater system.

Three contaminants expected in desalter effluent are free hydrocarbon, sulfides, and ammonia. Multiple stages of treatment are used to reduce contaminant levels.

The main purpose of desalters is to remove salts from crude oil. Salts that remain in crude will lead to corrosion of refinery equipment. The salts can also cause plugging and fouling.
 - Typical desalter wash water rates are 5-8% of crude throughput. [1]
 - Two-stage electrical desalters can achieve crude salt concentration of 0.1 p.t.b. (lbs per thousand barrels of crude).
