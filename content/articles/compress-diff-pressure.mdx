Centrifugal Compressor dP

How is the differential pressure of a compressor predicted? What information do you need?

At minimum, you will need mechanical characteristics of the compressor such as the number of wheels, diameter of the wheels, and the rotor speed.

However, this will allow for predicting the polytropic feet of head, which is not the same as the differential pressure.

To predict a differential pressure, you also need gas density.

Differential pressure is the product of polytropic feet of head and vapor density.

✅ Vapor density and polytropic feet of head are not related.
✅ Changing the vapor density does not change the polytropic feet of head.
✅ The reverse is also true.

Norman Liberman explains this concept in the excellent book, “A Working Guide to Process Equipment”.
